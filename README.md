# Distributed Computing And Information Causality

Bachelor Thesis of Simon Pfeiffer at the Quantum Computing Group at TU Darmstadt.

The Mathematica notebook is used for the classification of Boolean functions of two bits in [arXiv:2602.10206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.10206). In particular, it enumerates all $(f_{00},f_{01},f_{10},f_{11})$, including $f_{00}=x_0$ and $f_{00}=x_0x_1$, and confirms the eight classes.
